# Housing Market Analysis | Tableau

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard exploring residential housing prices and the property characteristics associated with them.

Using a dataset of **1,460 residential properties**, the analysis examines sale price distributions, building types, living area, house styles, and overall property quality.

The objective is to identify meaningful housing market patterns and communicate findings through clear, interactive data visualizations.

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Distribution of Home Sale Prices
A histogram illustrating the frequency distribution of residential property sale prices.

**Purpose:** Identify common price ranges, distribution patterns, and high-priced outliers.

### 2. Property Distribution by Building Type
A bar chart showing the number and percentage of properties across building categories.

**Purpose:** Understand the composition of residential property types.

### 3. Living Area vs. Sale Price
A scatter plot with a linear trend line examining the relationship between above-ground living area and property sale price.

**Purpose:** Explore the association between property size and market value.

### 4. Median Sale Price by Overall Quality
A bar chart comparing median sale prices across overall quality ratings.

**Purpose:** Evaluate how typical sale prices vary with property quality.

### 5. Sale Price Distribution by House Style
A box-and-whisker visualization comparing sale price distributions across different architectural house styles.

**Purpose:** Examine differences in median prices, variability, and outliers.

## Key Insights

**1. Single-Family Homes Dominate the Dataset**

Single-family detached homes account for **83.56% (1,220 properties)** of the 1,460 residential properties analyzed.

**2. Housing Prices Are Right-Skewed**

Most home sales are concentrated around the **$100K–$250K range**, while a smaller number of properties have substantially higher sale prices.

**3. Larger Living Areas Are Associated with Higher Sale Prices**

The scatter plot indicates a positive relationship between above-ground living area and sale price. However, homes with similar living areas can have considerably different selling prices.

**4. Overall Quality Is Strongly Associated with Sale Price**

Median sale prices increase consistently across quality ratings, from approximately **$50K at rating 1** to **$432K at rating 10**.

**5. House Styles Exhibit Different Pricing Patterns**

The box plots reveal differences in sale price distributions across house styles, including variability and high-priced outliers.
